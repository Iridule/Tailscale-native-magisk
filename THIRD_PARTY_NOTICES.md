does not contain the Tailscale executables themselves.

At installation or manual update time, the module retrieves patched Android
prebuilt binaries from:

- [`android-kxxt/external_tailscale_prebuilt`](https://github.com/android-kxxt/external_tailscale_prebuilt)

licenses and notices. The MIT license in this repository applies to this
module's integration scripts and documentation, not to third-party software.
